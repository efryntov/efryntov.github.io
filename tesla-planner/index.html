PERSONAL ANDROID PROTOTYPE

Tesla Shift Planner

Plan one-time preconditioning departures around changing work shifts. Review departures before synchronizing them to your vehicle.

This personal prototype uses Tesla’s official Fleet API. It is independent of Tesla, Inc.

This static website hosts the application’s public key and login callback. It does not ask for your Tesla password, store access tokens, or send vehicle commands.
